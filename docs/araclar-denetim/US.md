### Checked against its source and found right

- **Albumin categories at 30 and 300 mg/g, and the GFR categories.** The code calls 30 to 300 mg/g A2 and above 300 A3; so does Table 3 of KDIGO 2024 ([KDIGO_FULL](https://kdigo.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/KDIGO-2024-CKD-Guideline.pdf)). The first pass's doubt about the 300 boundary is closed: the code is right.
- **DAS28.** Both formulas (with ESR, with CRP), coefficient for coefficient; CRP in mg/L; the four bands with their limits, which are those of the American College of Rheumatology's 2019 table ([DAS_ALT](https://www.das-score.nl/en/das-and-das28/how-to-meaure-the-das28/how-to-calculate-the-das28/alternative-validated-formulae), [DAS_CRP_CALC](https://www.das-score.nl/das28/DAScalculators/DAS28_CRP_4VAR.html), [ACR_RA_2019](https://rheumatology.org/api/asset/blt65fc8b2649e03455)).
- **The 28 joints** of the joint count ([DAS_JOINTS](https://www.das-score.nl/en/das-and-das28/how-to-meaure-the-das28/twenty-eight-joints)).
- **PASI formula**: weights, the 0 to 4 signs, the 0 to 6 area score, the 0 to 72 range ([CADTH_PASI](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK534046/)).
- **EASI for a patient of 8 or over**: weights, area table, range ([HOME_EASI_GUIDE](https://www.homeforeczema.org/documents/easi-user-guide-jan-2017-v3.pdf)).
- **SCORAD formula** and its maximum of 103 ([ORANJE](https://repub.eur.nl/pub/35156)).
- **Expected height**: the 13 cm (5 in) correction ([AAFP](https://www.aafp.org/afp/2015/0701/p43.pdf)).
- **Hearing grades from 26 dB upwards** match the ASHA table, and a four-frequency average is one of the two US conventions ([ASHA](https://asha.org/public/hearing/degree-of-hearing-loss), [NIDCD_PTA](https://www.nidcd.nih.gov/health/statistics/what-numbers-mean-epidemiological-perspective-hearing)).
- **PSA rate of change with no threshold** fits the urologists' guideline, which says velocity must not be the sole reason for a further test ([AUA_EDPC](https://www.auanet.org/documents/Guidelines/PDF/2026%20Guidelines/EDPC%202026%20Unabridged%20FINAL.pdf)).
- **Dose volume rounded to 0.1 mL**: no US source read states a rounding step, for or against. The white paper asks for a device graduated finely enough for the amount ([NCPDP_ML](https://www.fda.gov/media/88498/download)).

## The findings that matter most

1. **Kidney tool (internal medicine): referral flags attributed to KDIGO that KDIGO 2024 does not state that way.** The screen says "KDIGO criterion for referral to a nephrologist" for any albumin ratio above 300 mg/g, for a GFR fall of more than 25% in a year, and for "the very-high-risk cell". A doctor is told a guideline says something it does not.
2. **Both kidney tools show a risk colour when the urine test is missing.** eGFR 75 with nothing else typed reads "Low risk (green cell)". The cell is defined by two results; with one missing the tool silently assumes the better category.

## What the kit lacks to apply these decisions for the United States alone

Read in `countries/_dil/en/araclar/index.ts`, `countries/_dil/en/girdi.ts`, `lib/ulke/araclar/tipler.ts` and `countries/us/ayarlar.ts`. A country that takes the shared English set can today do exactly three things to a tool: switch it off (`kapali`), change words on it (`degisen`), and rename a unit. Everything else below is missing. And before any of it: **none of the core set exists in the kit's catalogue** — each new tool needs its arithmetic written once in the kit (country-neutral, with its published source) before any country can switch it on.

1. **A country cannot say who sees a tool.** The roles of every tool are written once in the shared English set for all five countries. The US pack cannot show the kidney tool to family medicine, the hearing tool to audiologists, or the follow-up list to everybody.
2. **A country cannot have a tool of its own.** The pack's tool list is assembled by the shared set, and what a country hands the set (`EnAraclarGirdisi`) has no field for a tool of its own. A US-only tool (PREVENT, the Medicare wellness-visit list, the mammography statements) would have to be added to the shared set and then switched off by hand in the other four countries.
3. **A country cannot change a tool's numbers or band names** unless the kit left them open as parameters, and only eight slot tools do. The hearing-loss grades, the expected-height range and the return-to-sport steps are fixed in the kit. Needed: country parameters or country variants for those three.
4. **A country cannot add, split or merge a role, only rename one.** The 40 keys are shared by five countries and a test holds every pack to exactly those 40. Ten specialties and eight professions to add, one role to split (heart and vascular surgery) and one to fold away (clinic dermatology) all wait on this.
5. **A role is one thing; here it is two.** A nurse practitioner or physician assistant has a profession *and* works in a specialty. The account has one role, and every doctor role's instruction to the model begins "You are an experienced attending physician".
6. **Tools are gated by role only, never by the patient's age.** The adult core tools would show for pediatrics and children's tools for adult medicine. The branch rule of this product (one specialty's content must not show for another) applies to age the same way.
7. **Units inside a tool.** No height in feet and inches; no weight field that stays in kilograms when the country's unit is pounds; no unit choice on a single field (CRP in mg/L or mg/dL). The first two were recorded by the earlier audit; the third is new.
8. **No place for data with a date.** Growth-chart tables, the immunization schedules, the diagnosis codes and the USPSTF list are not text and not arithmetic: they are published data sets with a release date and a credit line. The kit has no shape for "a dated data set from a named source", the same gap the earlier audit found for medicine names.
9. **No place for a rights holder's notice or a licence state.** A tool has one citation line. PHQ-9's credit line, the ACR's trademark notice, or "licensed from … until …" have nowhere to live, and a slot cannot record that its licence was asked for or granted.
10. **No state.** Consent ages, recording consent and return-to-play rules differ by state; the pack has one value for the country.
