## WhatsApp — iPhone / Android readability

Copy-paste; preview on your phone **before** send (bold + list).

| Do | Don’t |
|----|--------|
| `*bold*` (no spaces inside the `*`) | Leading `*` bullets (collides with bold) |
| Bullets: `- ` + short line (native WA list) | `•` or one long wrapping line |
| Blank line before/after a list | Indented continuation under a bullet |
| Links: `www.au.notya.io/...` (**no** `https://`) | `https://` — triggers a big OG card |
| Dismiss the OG card with **X**, then send | Send with the card attached |
| One blank line between sections | 3–4 blank lines (WA collapses them) |

**Quiet hours (AU):** no cold WhatsApp after **20:00** in the recipient’s state. Prefer ~**09:00–19:00** local.

**Greeting:** `Hi Dr {FirstName},` — never Turkish “Hocam”. For a practice mobile (no named doctor): `Hi {PracticeName} team,`.

## Specialty Araçlar blocks (Australian English — AU pack)

Swap `*{Specialty heading}*` + the five bullets. Names match AU clinical tools (`countries/au/araclar/…`), not Turkish titles.

### Paediatrics

```
*In paediatrics*
- WHO growth percentiles
- National Immunisation Program schedule and catch-up
- Westley croup score
- Maintenance fluid volume (Holliday–Segar)
- Paediatric report (growth, exam, immunisation status)
```

### Obstetrics & gynaecology (KD)

```
*In obstetrics and gynaecology*
- CTG: normal or abnormal features (RANZCOG)
- Obstetric / gynaecological report
- Structured ultrasound findings in the note
- Consent discussion record for procedures
- Follow-up prompts around postnatal review windows
```

### Dermatology

```
*In dermatology*
- Dermoscopy ABCD rule (Total Dermoscopy Score)
- Seven-point dermoscopy checklist
- SALT score (alopecia areata)
- Fitzpatrick skin phototype
- Dermoscopy and skin examination report
```

### Clinical dietetics (APD)

```
*For Accredited Practising Dietitians*
- ADIME notes (assessment through evaluation)
- MST score record
- Energy and protein starting estimates (kJ)
- IDDSI levels (shared with speech pathology when needed)
- Patient portal clients actually open between reviews
```
