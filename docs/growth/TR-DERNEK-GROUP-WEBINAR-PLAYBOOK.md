## 1. Hard truth about Instagram “groups”

What you are seeing on Instagram are almost always **dernek / kongre / klinik sayfaları** (public profiles), not Facebook-style open groups where anyone can post.

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Can Notya post *into* Marmara Pediatri’s account and hit all ~3k followers? | **No.** Only their admins publish to that feed. |
| Do all followers see every post? | **No.** IG algo; typical organic reach ≪ follower count. |
| Can Gökhan “speak to the group” via IG alone? | Only if **they** host him (live, Collab Reel/Post, Stories, Broadcast Channel) or you buy paid reach. |
| Is follower count = webinar audience? | **No.** Followers ≠ registered attendees. Treat IG as **discovery + trust**, webinar/kongre as **conversion**. |

**Usable IG mechanics (in order of trust):**

1. **Dernek-hosted Live / webinar promo** — they announce; Gökhan joins Zoom/Meet from US.
2. **Collab post / Reel** — both `@notya` and `@dernek` co-author → appears on both profiles.
3. **Guest Reel** they film/edit — soft-sell free trial at end.
4. **Story takeover / Q&A sticker** — short window, high engagement.
5. **Broadcast Channel** (if they have one) — one-way blast to subscribers.
6. Paid boost of *their* post (they remain publisher).

Cold comments / DMs into member chats look spammy and burn nonprofit goodwill. Skip.

## 2. Why this channel beats 1:1 WA (when done right)

| Mode | Typical reach / session | Trust | Cost shape |
|------|-------------------------|-------|------------|
| 1:1 WA | 1 doctor | Low–medium | Time (anti-ban, personalization) |
| Dernek evening webinar | 15–80 live | High (peer CME frame) | Prep + Zoom; often **€0 cash** if education-first |
| Regional şube meeting | 30–120 | High | Travel or remote slot |
| National kongre uydu / firma sunumu | 80–300 room | Medium (branded) | **High** (pharma-scale TL) |
| National kongre stand | Foot traffic | Medium | High + staffing |

**Best fit for Notya now:** education-first **dernek webinars + regional meetings**, with Gökhan as *pediatrist teaching peers*, Notya as free tool (no card). Buy expensive kongre packages later for brand, not for first demos.

Notya is **health software**, not a TİTCK-ruhsatlı ilaç. Pharma “uydu sempozyum” rules do not map 1:1 — but dernek boards still hate hard sells. Frame every ask as **mesleki eğitim**.

## 4. Playbook: how to get Gökhan in front of members

### Path A — Dernek-hosted webinar (primary, lowest friction)

**Offer them:**

- Peer speaker (Uzm. Dr. / pediatrist — title as they prefer).
- Clinical topic first; product last 5 minutes + free trial (no CC).
- Full remote (Zoom/Meet); recording optional for their archive.
- Co-branded title slide: Dernek logo + “bilimsel seminer” (Notya logo smaller or only on last slide).
- You handle Zoom, registration (Cal.com / Notya form), reminder SMS/WA to *registrants only*.
- They handle: 1 IG post + Stories + member email/WhatsApp blast if they have a list.

**Soft-sell script (last 5 min):**  
“Kullandığımız araç Notya — Türk hekimleri için; ücretsiz deneme, kart istemiyor. Linki dernek sayfasında / chat’te.”

### Path B — Collab content without a live slot

If board is not ready for a webinar: 1 Collab Reel (problem → 60s demo → “ücretsiz dene”) filmed by Gökhan, edited clean, posted as Collab. Still better than 1:1 WA for reach.

### Path C — Regional / şube evening meetings

TJOD and others already run **city şube** scientific evenings (e.g. Malatya, Balıkesir, İstanbul day meetings). Same Path A pitch, smaller room, often faster yes. Gökhan remote on a big screen works.

## 6. Nonprofit-safe packaging (smart marketing)

Derneks are nonprofit and reputation-sensitive. What works:

1. **Education title, product subtitle** — never the reverse.
2. **Unrestricted grant / “koşulsuz eğitim desteği”** language if you pay for Zoom ads or coffee — money to *dernek*, not to individual doctors.
3. **Gift assets they can own:** Pediatri / KD / Derm pratik kart PDF for members (you already built these) — “üyelerinize ücretsiz kart; isterseniz semineri de yapalım.”
4. **Disclose** Gökhan’s relation to Notya on slide 2 (çıkar çatışması) — mirrors TPK / TTB culture.
5. **No pharma-style gifts**, no paid attendance lists bought under the table.
6. **Free trial, no CC** as the only CTA — matches “üye yararı.”

What burns the channel: blasting “Notya satın al” into member WhatsApp groups; fake “CME” that is only a pitch; scraping member lists from IG.
