# How TR medical derneks actually work (ped / KD / derm / KBB)

Researched 24 Nov 2026 from public sites, Doktorclub directory (last check dates Sep–Nov 2026), TTB-UDEK membership list, congress pages, and Instagram post patterns (webinar flyers, evening meetings, congress Reels). Instagram follower counts change daily — treat them as **order of magnitude**, not CRM fields.

**Classification:** Turkey-only growth GTM. Not doctor-app spine. Country: EXEMPT.

## B. Operating model (same across branşlar)

Studied from: TMPD regional eğitim posts, TDD Dermatonline webinar PDFs, TJOD şube hotel programs, KBB “akşam toplantısı” and Foniatri seminer duyuruları, Marmara congress theme, TURKDERM monthly olgu, PUADER olgu sempozyum, Neonatoloji RSV webinar 21 Kas 2026.

### 1. Who decides

- **Başkan + genel sekreter** green-light scientific content.
- **Sekreterya / paid secretary / mali müşavir** (Çocuk Endokrin lists Erdal İnce; many derneks use a PCO for kongre) answers email first.
- **Çalışma grubu başkanı** (TDD) can slot a Tuesday 19:30 webinar without waiting for ulusal kongre bilimsel kurul.
- **Kongre bilimsel kurul** is a **12–18 month** calendar. Do not ask Marmara 2027 for a speaker slot as the first email.

### 2. What they post on Instagram (pattern)

Almost every active dernek IG is a **broadcast channel**, not a discussion group:

1. Flyer of next evening meeting / webinar (date, Zoom/hotel, “üyelerimiz davetlidir”).
2. Congress aftermovie / opening konuşması Reel (TPK `@turkpediatri`).
3. Bayram / 24 Kasım öğretmenler günü (KBB posted this today).
4. Hasta-eğitimi carousel (Rinoloji: nazal polip, antihistaminik).
5. Rare: Collab with another dernek.

**Implication:** comments are not a sales channel. The flyer is proof they **already know how to fill a room**. Your job is to become the *next flyer*, not to hijack theirs.

### 3. Typical live formats (book these, in this order)

| Format | Length | When | Decision speed | Fit for Gökhan-from-US |
|--------|--------|------|----------------|------------------------|
| Dernek Zoom / Dermatonline | 45–90 dk, 19:00–21:00 TR | Weeknights | Days–2 weeks | **Best** |
| Şube otel akşam toplantısı | 90–180 dk + yemek | Weeknights | 2–6 weeks | Remote on projector works |
| Aylık olgu (TURKDERM, PUADER) | 60–120 dk | Recurring | Fast if they have a hole | Good |
| Bölgesel eğitim (TMPD 103/104) | Half day | Rolling | Months | Good if already scheduled |
| Ulusal kongre panel | 15–20 dk talk | Annual | 6–12 months | Secondary |
| Uydu / firma sunumu | Branded | Annual | Paid TL | Avoid until trials proven |

### 4. Money and ethics

- Nonprofit. Pharma funds kongre via **koşulsuz destek** to the *dernek*, TİTCK-notified for drugs. Notya is **software**, not a ruhsatlı ürün — still: no gifts to doctors, no buying member lists, slide-2 çıkar çatışması (Gökhan ↔ Notya).
- TPK has a published hekim–endüstri kılavuzu. Mirror it: education first, product last 5 minutes, free trial no CC.
- PCO emails (`info@millipediatri2026.org`, `ekdd2026@flaptour.com.tr`) sell **stands**. Use only after a scientific relationship.

### 5. Remote US speaker

Precedent is strong: Milli Pediatri 70. already billed international faculty; Marmara 9. Kongre (2022) was fully online; TDD webinars are native Zoom/Dermatonline; Çocuk Nefroloji has run e-kongre. **Zoom from the US is not a novelty.** Offer 20:00 TR (12:00–13:00 US East).

## C. Branş-by-branş

### Pediatrics

**Power centers:** TMPD (Ankara, national + bölge toplantıları + podcast `@turkiyemillipediatridernegi`), TPK (İstanbul, 1930, `@turkpediatri`, Genç Pediatristler), Marmara (İstanbul university orbit, 2027 pratik theme), PUADER (İzmir, temsilciler in 4 cities, `@izmirpediatri`).

**Subspecialty that still books general-ped talks:** Neonatoloji (webinar-native), CAIAD, Çocuk Endokrin (has a **sosyal medya takımı** — rare and useful), Çocuk Nöroloji, Nefroloji, Gastro.

**Skip first:** hemato/onko/kardiyo/acil — Gökhan is not that subspecialist.

**IG reality:** many “pediatri” accounts are ABD intern-run. They will not answer a speaker RFP. Use them later for Collab if a hoca intros.

**Marmara deep-dive:** Başkan Figen Akalın; kongre başkanı Nurdan Yıldız; genel sekreter Elif Karakoç Aydıner. Theme 2027 = pratik. First ask = member webinar now, not March 2027 stage.

### KD / jinekoloji

**Power center:** TJOD (`@tjodorg`, `info@tjod.org`). Şube list webpage is **down** (“GÜNCELLENMEKTEDİR”) — discover şubeler from **meeting posts**: İstanbul, İstanbul Anadolu, Bursa, Malatya, Balıkesir, Gaziantep, Hatay, Antalya, Samsun, Kayseri, Sivas, Şanlıurfa, İzmir, Ankara.

**How şubeler work:** 18:00–21:00 hotel, 3–4 talks, local profs, sometimes joint TUBİD/PERIDER/JED. Perfect for a 20-min remote module once they trust the topic.

**TJOD İstanbul:** Başkan Engin Oral; scientific Sundays (ör. 8 Mart 2026 ürojinekoloji). Email `dernek@tjodistanbul.org` — **not** his private clinic first.

**TJOV** (`@tjovorg`, `info@tjov.org.tr`) and **Perinatoloji** (`info@perinatal.org.tr`, congress IG `@ulusalperinatolojikongresi`) are the gebelik-story homes.

**Gökhan is a pediatrist.** For KD, always offer a **KD co-host**. Solo Gökhan on a TJOD night will be questioned.

### Dermatology

**Two nationals:** TDD (Ankara, UDEK, `@turkdermatolojidernegi`, Dermatonline **factory** of weeknight webinars) and TURKDERM (İstanbul, monthly olgu, `turkderm@turkderm.org.tr`).

**TDD post pattern:** “Çalışma Grubu Webinarı, Perşembe 20:00, Dermatonline Yayın Linki.” Speakers are almost all academic dermatologists. To get Gökhan in, pitch **workflow/izlem**, not a disease lecture, and invite a TDD dermatologist as chair.

**EKDD** congress 18–22 Kas 2026 — do not interrupt this week.

**City derm derneks** (Ankara, Akdeniz, Çukurova, Anadolu, Doğu): smaller, often hungrier for program. Find emails next.

**IG note (Turkderm 2024 paper):** popular *personal* derm IGs are 85% private practice, cosmetic-heavy. Those are **not** derneks. Do not confuse them with TDD.

### KBB

**Two UDEK nationals:** Türk KBB-BBC Derneği (`kbb@kbb.org.tr`, Yenibosna HQ) and Ankara KBB-BBC (`@kbbbbcdernegi`, kbbbbcder.org).  
**İstanbul KBB uzmanları** share the **same phone/building** as national (`0212 234 44 81`, Nish İstanbul). Published `info@istanbulkbb.org` has **no MX and no A** (bounced 26 Kas 2026). Live path: `tkbbd@kbb.org.tr` (Yandex membership inbox). Do not send a second copy to `kbb@kbb.org.tr`.

**Operating pattern:** kadavra courses, Foniatri seminerleri (25 Kas 2026), Doğu Marmara akşam toplantısı, Mersin KBB Günleri Şub 2027. Instagram used since 2022 for duyuru.

**Rinoloji** (`@turkrinolojidernegi`, `info@rinoturk.org`) posts patient education — more consumer IG, still a board that runs a kongre.

**Gökhan is not KBB.** Same rule as KD: **co-host a KBB uzman** or wait. Email still goes today with that offer.

## D. What “book webinars today” can mean

| Today | This week | This month |
|-------|-----------|------------|
| 20 board emails + HubSpot tasks | 2–5 replies; 1 PUADER/şube/TDD CG maybe holds a date | 2–4 live webinars if Gökhan slots are real |
| 16 IG admin DMs matching those emails | Phone sekreterya D+3 | Collab Reels from recordings |

Nobody reputable will put 80 doctors on Zoom tonight because a cold email arrived at 17:39. Anyone who would is not a dernek.
