# ESC guidelines in the protocol registry — research log (KAYNAK-BIRLESTIRME-ESC)

Research date: **2026-11-26** (Claude, for Kaan). Registry: `lib/kaynaklar/protokolKutuphanesi/uluslararasi.ts` (`ESC_BELGELERI`).
Why ESC: the Türk Kardiyoloji Derneği (TKD) endorses, translates and distributes ESC guidelines; they are the cardiology practice
authority in Türkiye (TURKISH_REFS kardiyoloji, line 1). Kaan's rule: always the latest edition.

## Sources read

- **ESC — "All ESC Practice Guidelines"** (official listing), read in full 2026-11-26:
  https://www.escardio.org/guidelines/clinical-practice-guidelines/all-esc-practice-guidelines/ — 31 entries, none marked
  superseded. Every year below is the year in the title the listing gives (`resmi-liste`). This page is the only URL the monthly
  edition check reads for ESC (`yalnizTakipUrl`), so a new / revised ESC guideline shows up as one founder-review item.
- ESC guideline pages for the four 2026 documents (heart failure, CVD & CKD, cardiac rehabilitation, Fifth Universal Definition
  of MI): published 28 August 2026, European Heart Journal; DOIs read on the pages / ACC.org report
  (https://www.acc.org/Latest-in-Cardiology/Articles/2026/08/29/08/48/mon-guidelines-esc-2026): ehag100 (HF), ehag098 (CKD,
  developed with ERA per ACC; the ESC page names no co-sponsor), ehag099 (cardiac rehabilitation), ehag101 (UDMI — joint
  ESC/ACC/AHA/WHF consensus; the page does not state the status of the 2018 Fourth Definition).
- 2026 heart-failure page: lists only the 2026 guideline; the 2021 guideline and its focused update are no longer listed.
- 2023 HF focused update (for the superseded entry): ESC page
  https://www.escardio.org/guidelines/clinical-practice-guidelines/focused-update-on-heart-failure-guidelines/ — "2023 Focused
  Update of the 2021 ESC Guidelines for the diagnosis and treatment of acute and chronic heart failure" (EHJ DOI ehad195).

## Turkish (TKD) versions

TKD's publications page (https://tkd.org.tr/en/menu/14/publications) says the Society has endorsed, translated and published 33
ESC guidelines and pocket guidelines (since 2011) "available at www.tkd.org.tr", but no per-guideline list could be read, and the
searches returned only ESC's own Turkish patient leaflet for AF 2024 (ESC copyright, no TKD imprint) and TKD bulletin
summaries. **No TKD Turkish edition is confirmed for any guideline below** — the registry says so in each entry's note, and
the old static "ESC/TKD <year>" wording was removed. Next pass: OPEN-COMMITMENTS TR-PROTOKOL-KUTUPHANESI-06b.

## Citation lock

`lib/doktor/kaynakKilidi.ts` `protokolKaynakTemizle`: "ESC <year>" / "<year> ESC" (partner codes ESC/EACTS, ESC/ERS, ESC/EAS,
ESC/TKD kept) survives only when a current registry ESC document — or its focused update — has that year AND one of its topic
words is in the same clause. "ESC 2024 Atriyal Fibrilasyon" stays; "ESC 2019 AF", "ESC 2021 KY", "ESC 2023 KY" lose the year.
