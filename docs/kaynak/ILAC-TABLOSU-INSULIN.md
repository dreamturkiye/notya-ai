## Levemir (insülin detemir) — status
- Novo Nordisk is ending Levemir production — portfolio consolidation, explicitly not a safety or quality issue.
- USA: FlexPen discontinued 01.04.2024, whole brand 31.12.2024 (PharmExec, 14.11.2023).
- UK: Penfill and FlexPen supply expected until end of December 2026 (Novo Nordisk UK letter agreed with MHRA/DHSC, October 2025); Ireland (HSE) and Australia (ACSQHC fact sheet, 2026-10) publish the same withdrawal.
- **Türkiye:** on 2026-11-25 ilacabak shows LEVEMIR FLEXPEN 100U/ML 5X3ML (barcode 8699676950799) with a price update of 21.11.2026 and ilacrehberi lists it "Piyasada Var". **No Türkiye-specific TİTCK withdrawal notice was found.** The row is marked `piyasaDurumu.durum = 'cekiliyor'` with that exact wording; the name still resolves to insülin detemir and the card says to plan a switch to another basal insulin with the doctor.
