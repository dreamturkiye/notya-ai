# Reported medicines at the pharmacy — preparation note (NOTYA-SUT-RAPOR-01)

Written 2026-10-10, updated the same day after the official text was read. **Nothing in this note is built.** It says what the current SUT contains on reported medicines and family-medicine authority, and what would change in the Turkish product on the day a new regulation is published. Open items: `docs/OPEN-COMMITMENTS.md`, section NOTYA-SUT-RAPOR-01.

Source for every article below: the SGK consolidated Sağlık Uygulama Tebliği downloaded from www.sgk.gov.tr on 2026-10-10 ("02.10.2026 - Değişiklik Tebliği İşlenmiş Güncel 2013 SUT", amendment of Resmî Gazete 2026-10-02 no. 33388 worked in), branch `kaynak/sut-2026-10-02`. The Minister's statements of 2026-10-10 are news, not text; nothing here rests on them.

## Four questions, answered from the text

**1. Collecting reported medicines for the life of the report without a new prescription.** The text does not provide it, monthly or otherwise. What it provides is SUT 4.1.6(1): for medicines on the co-payment-exempt list (EK-4/D), a doctor in a branch entitled to issue the report may write a prescription for up to one year of use, and the pharmacy dispenses it "en fazla üçer aylık dozda" for as long as that prescription runs. A prescription is still needed, once a year at most, and the report must be valid. Left out of this: coloured-prescription medicines, off-label use, medicines brought from abroad, the medicines of SUT 4.2.1, 4.2.10 and 4.2.14, and any SGK names. People posted abroad on permanent duty may receive one year at once (4.1.6(4)).

**2. What a family physician may prescribe on a specialist's report, and which reports a family medicine specialist may issue or renew.**
- Prescribing on a report (SUT 4.1.1(8)): the medicines of SUT 4.2.13.1 and 4.2.13.2 (hepatitis B and D), 4.2.15.B, C, Ç, D-1, D-2, E and F (the medicines of that article other than klopidogrel), 4.2.20 (palivizumab) and 4.2.38 (diabetes) that an internal medicine or child health specialist may prescribe on a health report may also be prescribed on that report by contracted or authorised family physicians and by family medicine specialists. More generally, SUT 4.1.1(10): a medicine prescribed on a health report may be prescribed on that report by every doctor unless the text says otherwise; and 4.1.1(8), first sentence: a contracted family physician may prescribe whatever a family medicine specialist may.
- Without a report, limited quantities: named cases only, such as artificial tears (4.2.33.D(2)) and angiotensin receptor blockers (EK-4/F 51). The lists stay in the text and are not copied into the product.
- Reports a family medicine specialist may issue or renew: later klopidogrel reports after the first two report periods, at most 12 months each (4.2.15.A(2)-(3)); second and later coeliac reports (4.2.16(6)); continuation reports for high-dose statins after the first 24-month report (4.2.28.A-1(4)); the specialist report for oestrogens, hormone replacement and progestogens (4.2.29(1)); the specialist report for repaglinid, nateglinid and combined oral antidiabetics (4.2.38(2)); the report for blood glucose test strips (3.3.2(1)(a)).
- Not theirs: the anticoagulant reports of 4.2.15.D. After the first 24 months a family physician may prescribe on the specialist report, but does not issue it.

**3. Report duration ceilings in general.** SUT 4.1.3(5): a health report for medicines is valid for at most two years, special arrangements apart. SUT 4.1.8(2): an EK-4/F report is valid for the length of treatment, never beyond two years. SUT 3.1.2.2(2): the same two years for medical materials in continuous use. The special arrangements run both ways: shorter fixed terms for many medicines (three months, six months, one year) and three years for coeliac disease and inborn metabolic disease (4.2.16). An off-label approval with no stated term cannot run past one year (4.1.4(4)(c)). A report stays valid to the end of its term after a SUT change if it meets the new rule (4.1.3(9)). The 2025 amendment lengthened several six-month terms to one year (DOAK health board reports, artificial tears, antidepressants without a specialist).

**4. Would a medicine-report draft tool be within a family medicine specialist's authority?** Partly. The text lets a family medicine specialist issue or renew the reports listed under question 2, most of them renewals after another specialist's first report. A tool limited to those would be within authority. A copy of the dahiliye tool would not: its anticoagulant template is outside family medicine authority, for most diabetes medicines the text names other branches, and for hypertension and lower-dose statins it says "uzman hekim raporu" without naming a branch, which does not settle the matter for family medicine. Nothing was built; the decision is NOTYA-SUT-RAPOR-01d.

## On the day a new regulation is published

**Screens that show a report's validity**
- Araçlar › Hasta Raporları (`/doktor-tools/sgk-rapor`, every branch): "Rapor Süresi (Ay)", start and end date on the printed draft, the "Yasal / Medula notu".
- Branch report drafts: dahiliye, kardiyoloji, nefroloji, psikiyatri, KBB, göğüs hastalıkları, göz, dermatoloji, romatoloji, onkoloji.
- Notya holds a **draft**; the valid report is the Medula e-Rapor. Notya knows an end date only when the doctor produced the draft here.

**What a doctor would no longer need to do**
- Write a repeat prescription for an unchanged reported treatment. Today the text already allows one prescription a year for EK-4/D medicines (4.1.6(1)); the repeat-only visit through e-Reçete Asistanı and Muayene sonu paketi would become optional for more patients.
- Read the quantity warnings as they stand: the box-count and "rapor gerekebilir" hints in `lib/medula/receteHazirla.ts` and `lib/seansPaketi/sutKurallari.ts` must be re-read against the new text. The current text counts in months of dose, not boxes (NOTYA-SUT-RAPOR-01g).

**What a patient-portal reminder could say** (wording only after the official text; no medicine name, no diagnosis)
- "Raporlu ilacınız için raporunuz geçerli olduğu sürece yeni reçete gerekmeyebilir. Eczanenize danışın."
- "Raporunuzun süresi dolmadan kontrol randevunuzu alın."

**What must wait for the official text**
- Dose interval (monthly or three-monthly), which medicines and report types are covered, and from which date.
- Whether it applies to reports issued in private practice, and how Medula shows remaining supply.
- Who decides that the treatment is "unchanged", and what ends the entitlement.

## What the text means for the product today

- **Family medicine is a branch in Notya.** It sees the 12 shared tools (including Hasta Raporları and e-Reçete Asistanı) and four of its own: Aşı / tarama paketi, Kronik paket, Sevk / acil triyaj, kohort. It does not see the dahiliye "SGK ilaç raporu" draft tool. See question 4; the decision is Kaan's (classify before add: branch-only, not shared).
- **Drug information.** Seven of the assistant's SGK lines were corrected on 2026-10-10 against EK-4/E, EK-4/F and EK-4/A; the rest were checked and left. Details in `docs/OPEN-COMMITMENTS.md`.
- **Home reports.** Notya has no home-visit report flow. Hasta Raporları drafts work on a phone during a home visit; nothing changes.
