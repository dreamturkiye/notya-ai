## Items

### 1. HTI-5 final rule: DSI source-attribute requirements removed from health IT certification

- **What changed:** ASTP/ONC finalised "Health Data, Technology, and Interoperability: ONC Deregulatory Actions to Unleash Prosperity" (HTI-5). In § 170.315(b)(11) (decision support interventions), the criterion is revised and the "model card" requirements are removed: (b)(11)(iv) "source attribute support" and (b)(11)(v) "source attribute access and modification". ONC says it may consider restoring "source attributes transparency" in future rulemaking. § 170.315(a)(9) (clinical decision support) and (b)(9) (care plan) are removed. § 170.315(g)(7), (g)(9) and (e)(3) are removed from 1 January 2027. § 170.315(e)(1) (view, download, transmit) is revised. HHS says the comments on HTI-5's information-blocking proposals "remain under consideration", so this final rule does not finalise them.
- **Official reference:** HHS press release "HHS Finalizes HTI-5 Deregulatory Rule…", 26 Oct 2026, https://www.hhs.gov/press-room/hhs-finalizes-hti-5-deregulatory-rule.html (opened). ONC page https://healthit.gov/regulations/hti-rules/hti-5-final-rule/ (opened). Criteria table https://healthit.gov/resources/hti-5-final-rule-removals-or-revisions-of-certification-criteria (opened). Federal Register public-inspection copy, document 2026-21943, RIN 0955-AA09, https://public-inspection.federalregister.gov/2026-21943.pdf (opened; first 100,000 of 125,419 characters read; the DSI preamble section was not fully read).
- **In force / planned:** The rule takes effect 60 days after Federal Register publication (the date was still a placeholder in the public-inspection copy). Amendatory instructions 7, 10 and 12 take effect 1 January 2027. The revision to § 170.315(b)(1) takes effect 1 January 2030.
- **Touches in Notya (us):** Nothing today. Notya is not ONC-certified health IT, and the US legal drafts make no certification claim. It would matter only if Notya sought certification or a US EHR partner asked for DSI source attributes. Partners may now stop expecting model cards through certification.
- **Proposed action:** Note it in the US go-to-market file. Keep voluntary source-attribute transparency for the AI assistant as product policy, because ONC may restore it. Watch the information-blocking part, which is still pending.
- **Product change needed:** no
- **Urgency:** watch
