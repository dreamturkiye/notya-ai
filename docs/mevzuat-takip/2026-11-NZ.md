## Items

### 1. Manage My Health inquiry: compliance notices and final report (patient-portal breach)

- **What changed:** The Privacy Commissioner issued compliance notices to Manage My Health under HIPC rule 5(1)(a) and to Health NZ under rule 5(1)(b). The notices name seven areas where security protections were ineffective; the release names MFA effectiveness, restricted user access and control of unauthorised external access as already improved. Health NZ has until 29 January 2027 and Manage My Health until 31 August 2027. The final Phase 2 report sets general expectations for all agencies:
  - A customer who did not finish registration has not authorised further collection and storage of information in that account.
  - Authorisation requests must be clear.
  - Account holders should be contacted after 12 months of inactivity unless a law requires retention.
  - There should be a clear process for the personal representatives of deceased account holders.
  - Weak breach-identification processes are "not an excuse for delaying notification".
  - A processor that makes a design decision its client did not agree to "will be independently responsible for complying with the relevant privacy principles".
- **Official reference:**
  - OPC media release "Privacy Commissioner issues compliance notices to Manage My Health and Health NZ", 23 September 2026, https://www.privacy.org.nz/tuhono-connect/statements-media-releases/privacy-commissioner-issues-compliance-notices-to-manage-my-health-and-health-nz/ (opened; the notice PDFs themselves were not read).
  - OPC media release "Final report into Manage My Health inquiry a wake-up call for agencies", 30 October 2026, https://www.privacy.org.nz/tuhono-connect/statements-media-releases/final-report-into-manage-my-health-inquiry-a-wake-up-call-for-agencies/ (opened).
  - Inquiry page https://www.privacy.org.nz/focus-areas/manage-my-health-inquiry/ (opened).
  - Phase 2 report PDF https://www.privacy.org.nz/assets/DOCUMENTS/Manage-My-Health/20261030-Office-of-the-Privacy-Commissioner-Final-Manage-My-Health-Phase-2-report-A1197696.pdf (not opened).
- **In force / planned:** The notices are in force from 23 September 2026. The expectations in the report have applied since 30 October 2026 as the Commissioner's stated view of the existing law.
- **Touches in Notya (`nz`):**
  - Patient portal: registration, dormant and deceased accounts.
  - NZ privacy-policy draft (`countries/nz/yasal.ts`): breach notification, and Notya's role as service provider to doctors.
  - Doctor contracts or data-processing terms.
- **Proposed action:**

  - Put the dormant-account check (12 months), the deceased-holder process and the unfinished-registration purge on the portal backlog as a core item across all 40 branches.
  - Read the Phase 2 report and the rule 5 technical-standards page (could not be opened this run) before the backlog item is scoped.

### 3. HIPC Amendment No 2 (rule 3A / IPP3A) not named in the NZ legal draft

- **What changed:** Nothing changed in the period. Amendment No 2 to the Health Information Privacy Code 2020 was issued in March 2026 "to reflect new Information Privacy Principle 3A" and has been in force since 1 May 2026. It covers notification when personal information is collected indirectly. It is listed here because the NZ privacy draft names only "the Privacy Act 2020 and the Health Information Privacy Code" in general terms.
- **Official reference:** OPC, HIPC 2020 page, https://privacy.org.nz/privacy-act-2020/codes-of-practice/hipc2020/ (opened; the amendment PDF https://www.privacy.org.nz/assets/Codes-of-Practice-2020/Amendment-No-2-to-Health-Information-Privacy-Code-2020.pdf was title only, not opened).
- **In force / planned:** 1 May 2026.
- **Touches in Notya (`nz`):** The NZ privacy-policy draft. Collection through referral letters, uploaded documents and family members is indirect collection.
- **Proposed action:** Add "HIPC Amendment No 2 (rule 3A, in force 1 May 2026)" to the lawyer-review note of the NZ privacy draft.
