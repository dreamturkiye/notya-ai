## Items

### 1. Personal-data law rewritten on cross-border storage, adequacy list adopted, AI rules added; `uz` legal drafts do not reflect them

- **What changed:** (a) ZRU-1125 replaced Article 27-1 of the Law "On personal data". Only biometric data, genetic data and data of users of telecom operators in Uzbekistan must now be stored in Uzbekistan. Other personal data may be stored and processed abroad if one of three conditions is met: the country is on a Cabinet list of states with adequate protection; the operator uses standard contractual terms or binding corporate rules that meet the authorised body's requirements; or the operator follows international data-management standards on an approved list. The page shows no special rule for health data. (b) Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 415 approved the "Перечень иностранных государств, обеспечивающих адекватную защиту персональных данных". The lex.uz page that was opened did not show the annex. A news report (spot.uz, 04.08.2026; pointer only, not a basis) lists 49 states including the USA. The same report says breaches during transfer must be notified within 24 hours, with a full report within 72 hours, and that standard contractual clauses and binding corporate rules are due within three months. None of these three points was verified in the official text. (c) ZRU-1115 amends the Law "On informatization" with general rules on AI. Decisions with legal consequences for people's rights may not rest solely on AI-system conclusions, and unlawful processing of personal data using AI carries a fine of 50–100 BRV.
- **Official reference:** ZRU-1125, 26.03.2026, in force 27.03.2026, https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8104580 (opened). Cabinet Resolution No. 415, 29.07.2026, in force 03.08.2026, https://lex.uz/uz/docs/8371371 (opened; header only, no annex). The Uzbek full text https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8369688 was not opened (see below). ZRU-1115, 21.01.2026, https://lex.uz/ru/doc-passport/8011377 (opened; passport/summary page).
- **In force / planned:** All three were in force before the period. The follow-up contractual clauses / binding corporate rules would fall due about early November 2026, but that comes from news only and is unverified.
- **Touches in Notya (uz):** the legal drafts in `countries/uz/yasal.ts` (privacy notice, cookies, medical-device statement; uz-Latn, uz-Cyrl, ru). Their "for the lawyer" notes cite only ZRU-547 (linked to the English translation lex.uz/docs/4831939). They also bear on the clinical-note and assistant data flow to Anthropic (US-hosted), which now has a legal basis to check: the adequacy list, or contractual clauses.
- **Proposed action:** Add ZRU-1125, Cabinet Resolution No. 415 and ZRU-1115 to the lawyer-review notes in all three language packs of the `uz` drafts. Ask the lawyer to confirm three things against the official Uzbek text of No. 415: whether the USA is listed, whether health data falls under any localisation category (the opened text names only biometric, genetic and telecom-user data), and which breach-notification time limits apply. Do not write any compliance claim into the drafts.
- **Product change needed:** yes (legal-draft text only; no code logic). Logged in `docs/OPEN-COMMITMENTS.md`, waiting on Kaan.

### 2. State registration of medical and pharmaceutical workers (Decree UP-192) and its Cabinet implementing resolution

- **What changed:** The decree introduces state registration of medical and pharmaceutical workers. Rollout starts 1 July 2027 and is voluntary until 2030. From 2030 only registered persons may practise, with 5-year registration (para 2). Registration covers the specialties in the nomenclature of practice areas, and the Ministry of Health sets which specialties need registration, with or without a special exam (para 2(a)). Qualification categories are phased out (para 2(g)). A Unified Electronic Registry of specialists gets free public access (para 2(b)). The Cabinet must approve the registration Procedure by 1 April 2027 (para 8). The decree does not mention AI. News reports of 21.10.2026 (anhor.uz; pointer only) say a government resolution of 19.10.2026 approved the procedure, with the "Tibbiy malaka" platform and electronic-only certificates. That resolution's official text was not found on lex.uz.
- **Official reference:** Presidential Decree UP-192 "О внедрении системы проведения государственной регистрации медицинских и фармацевтических работников", 10.09.2026, National Database No. 06/26/192/0938, in force 16.09.2026, https://lex.uz/uz/docs/8481137 (opened, Russian text). The Uzbek page https://lex.uz/uz/docs/8487087 opened with header only. Cabinet resolution of 19.10.2026: number unknown, official text not opened (news only).
- **In force / planned:** decree from 16.09.2026; registration from 1 July 2027 (voluntary), mandatory from 2030.
- **Touches in Notya (uz):** nothing today. Later, possibly the `uz` specialty list, if the Ministry's list of registrable specialties changes names. A public registry might also later serve to verify doctors at sign-up.
- **Proposed action:** Watch for the Ministry of Health list of specialties subject to registration and the Cabinet Procedure. Compare specialty names with the 2026-10-10 audit (Order No. 6 nomenclature) when they appear.
- **Product change needed:** no
- **Urgency:** watch
