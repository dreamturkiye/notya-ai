## Items

### 1. Electronic informed consent (e-Nabız / SMS code)

- **What changed:** A new Ministry of Health regulation sets out how informed consent (bilgilendirilmiş rıza) may be taken electronically. The secondary summaries below agree on these points. There are two channels: e-Nabız, or the health facility's electronic system, both confirmed by a single-use SMS code tied to the specific procedure. The clinician must be registered in the Ministry's Entegre Kurumsal İşlem Platformu, and the patient's identity is verified before the process starts. A verified phone number is valid for 12 months. For minors and restricted patients the legal representative consents and must pass the same identity and phone verification; if that cannot be done electronically, consent is not taken electronically. Records carry the time, the procedure, the SMS verification record, the clinician, an integrity value and a timestamp. They go to the Ministry's central system and become visible to the patient on e-Nabız. The scope is first, second and third level facilities, which (per a law firm's reading) includes muayenehaneler and private polyclinics. Electronic consent is optional, not compulsory. The legal basis is 1219 sayılı Kanun art. 70, amended by 7557 sayılı Kanun art. 3 (RG 2025-07-24, no. 32965).
- **Official reference:** Resmî Gazete **2026-10-27**, item at `https://www.resmigazete.gov.tr/eskiler/2026/10/20261027-4.htm`. The title above comes from that official address in the search index. The issue number **33413** is given by a law firm and matches the sequence (2026-10-10 = 33396). **The official text itself could not be opened** (resmigazete.gov.tr refuses automated readers). Pointers, not the basis: medimagazin.com.tr (2026-10-27/28), hanyaloglu-acar.av.tr.
- **In force:** on publication, 2026-10-27 (per the secondary sources; to be confirmed in the text).
- **Touches in Notya:**
  - Sağlığım › Aşı Onamı (`app/portal/_components/AsiOnamView.tsx`, `app/portal/hasta/[token]/asi-onam/`). The parent confirms by typing name, relationship and phone. The record shows "Onaylandı · Uzaktan (Sağlığım)".
  - Remote consent methods `uzaktan-eposta` and `uzaktan-whatsapp` in `lib/asi/onam/` (`asiOnamMotoru.ts`, `asiOnamIcerigi.ts`).
  - Printable consent forms: `app/dashboard/doktor/onam/yazdir/`, `app/dashboard/doktor/asi-onam/yazdir/`, `app/doktor-tools/cc-onam-veli/`, `app/doktor-tools/plastik-onam/`, `app/ozel-branslar/cerrahi-onam/`. Paper consent is unaffected, because the regulation adds an electronic route and does not replace paper.
  - KVKK and legal pages (`app/kvkk/page.tsx`, `app/(yasal)/`) only if the consent flow changes.
- **Proposed action:**
  - (a) Get the official text read. Kaan can open the Resmî Gazete page in a browser and save it, or a run with access can read it.
  - (b) Against the text, decide whether Notya's remote confirmations may still be shown as consent given ("Onaylandı"). The alternative is to relabel them as a pre-visit information acknowledgement, with the binding consent taken in clinic on paper or through the e-Nabız/SMS route.
  - (c) Later and separately, decide whether Notya should ever connect to the Ministry's electronic consent route. That needs Ministry integration Notya does not have.
  - No code change until (a) is done.
- **Scope:** core (shared consent flows; the portal aşı onamı is pediatri-gated today, the printable forms are shared). Turkish law mechanics only (Ministry systems, e-Nabız), so it is exempt from six-country propagation under `.cursor/rules/ulke-paritesi-duzeltme.mdc`. Other countries keep their own consent law.
- **Urgency:** **now** for reading the text; **this month** for the labelling decision.

### 6. KVKK

- 2026-10-01 public announcement on how data controllers notify their reply to a data subject's request (`https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/9024/...`, read). The reply must be written or electronic, not oral (calls may only clarify the request). It should show acceptance or the reasons for refusal, the action taken and the delivery date. The 30-day limit of art. 13 stands. Notya's `app/kvkk/page.tsx` already states the 30 days. **No change needed.** Optionally, a sentence saying replies are given in writing or electronically could be added at Kaan's choice.
