# Data Processing Agreement — Notya Australia (template)

Drafted 2026-11-03 (AU-POLITIKA-01), pending review by an Australian health lawyer. Public summary:

final text.

**Parties:** Notya LLC, 3424 Sollers Point Rd., Baltimore, MD 21222, USA ("Notya") and the practice named below ("Practice").

1. **Purpose.** Notya provides the Notya practice management and clinical documentation service ("Service") to the Practice.
   This agreement covers personal information, including health information, that Notya handles for the Practice
   ("Practice Data").
2. **Roles.** The Practice decides what Practice Data is collected and why. Notya handles Practice Data only to provide the
   Service, on the Practice's documented instructions (these terms, the Practice's use of the Service and its written
   requests), and for no other purpose. Notya does not sell Practice Data, use it for advertising, or use it to train its
   own models.
3. **Confidentiality.** Notya limits access to people who need it to provide the Service and who are bound to confidentiality.
4. **Security.** Notya keeps the measures described on its Security and Data storage and retention pages (including
   application-level encryption of patient fields, per-practitioner access scoping, and storage in Sydney, Australia) and
   tells the Practice of a material reduction in them.
5. **Sub-processors.** The Practice authorises the providers listed at au.notya.io/sub-processors. Notya engages each under
   written terms requiring it to protect the information, remains responsible to the Practice for them, and gives at least
   30 days' email notice before adding or replacing a provider that receives patient information; the Practice may object
   and, if the objection cannot be resolved, end the Service with a pro-rata refund of prepaid fees for the unused period.
6. **Overseas disclosure.** Practice Data is stored in Sydney. Some providers process it overseas, as listed; Notya is a US
   company. Notya takes reasonable steps so overseas recipients do not breach the Australian Privacy Principles in relation
   to Practice Data (APP 8.1) and gives the Practice the information it needs for its own APP 8 position.
7. **Data breaches.** Notya notifies the Practice without undue delay, and within 72 hours, after confirming a data breach
   affecting Practice Data; cooperates in the assessment (within 30 days of suspicion); and, as agreed between the parties,
   notifies the OAIC and affected individuals where the breach is an eligible data breach.
8. **Requests.** Notya helps the Practice respond to access (APP 12) and correction (APP 13) requests and to complaints.
9. **Practice obligations.** The Practice has a lawful basis for collection (including consent for health information where
   required), confirms consent before recording a visit, reviews and approves every AI draft, and keeps its own records as
   state and territory law and professional rules require.
10. **Return and deletion.** On written request, or when the Service ends, Notya provides a copy of Practice Data and then
    deletes it from the live database within 30 days; backups expire on the provider's schedule. Notya may keep billing
    records the law requires it to keep.
11. **Information and audit.** Notya answers the Practice's reasonable written questions about its handling of Practice Data.
12. **Term.** This agreement lasts as long as Notya handles Practice Data for the Practice.
