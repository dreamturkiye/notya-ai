SOAP — doğum + first 24–48h lohusa.

Labor: partogram, Bishop, oxytocin, NST cat I–III, CS indication, VBAC counseling.

Emergencies: shoulder dystocia, atony 4T, eclampsia seizure algorithm — checklists, not a full EHR.

Birth note fields for the newborn handoff (bridge later): APGAR, weight, GBS, Hep B, vit K, heel-stick TSH/PKU reminder, breastfeeding, Anti-D logistics.

Lohusa 0–48h: NSD ≥24h / CS ≥48h discharge; bleeding, BP, uterus, breastfeeding, danger signs.

Cite Williams (obstetrik-gold) and Doğum Sonu Bakım (zorunlu-kamu) by role.
