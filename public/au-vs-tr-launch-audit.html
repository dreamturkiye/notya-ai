Pre-launch snapshot · 15 October 2026 · Australia against the Turkish product

Notya Australia is a clinic shell. Türkiye is the clinical product.

Compared au.notya.io/au (release/au 80672d2a) with notya.io (main c4d79aef). Scores are how much of each Turkish slice an Australian doctor can do today. 100 would mean the same job, in Australian words and under Australian law. No patient data.

Shell is live 65 tools, 10 of them Australia’s own, none signed No voice, no eRx, no PBS table, no ICD-10-AM
40Turkish specialty chapters with engines. Australia has 40 role names and shared note templates.
328Turkish engine files. Australia’s own arithmetic is 8 calculators plus 2 links.
145Tiles on the Turkish doctor tools grid. Australia switches on 65 country tools.
0Australian clinicians or lawyers who have signed the pack.

An invited general practitioner can open a day list, a patient, a visit, a calculator, an immunisation tick-list, a notifiable-disease name, and the Poisons Standard. That is a real product. It is not the product a Turkish doctor uses. The daily wow there is the voice assistant, the specialty note, the reimbursed medicine list, and a prescription that leaves the building. None of those exist for Australia. A launch can be an invited notes-and-reference beta. It cannot be advertised as the same Notya.

What a clinic will feel

Writing a prescription

Daily cost: the script is the visit.

Now. A dispensing-label checklist and a PBS link. Nothing is sent to a pharmacy.

Wow move. Either an eRx connection or a screen that says “this note is not a prescription” before the doctor looks for one.

The voice they were shown

Daily cost: typing the note.

Now. A core visit and a draft recording sentence. No assistant.

Wow move. The same visit loop as Türkiye, with an Australian voice, or a launch that does not promise one.

Catch-up vaccines

Daily cost: the child who is late.

Now. Milestone ticks. The Handbook catch-up text was not copied.

Wow move. Written permission from the Department, then a catch-up a paediatrician has read.

The national record

Daily cost: retyping what the patient already has.

Now. No My Health Record and no Healthcare Identifier. The identifier field is an optional Medicare card number, and a lawyer has not said it may be stored.

Wow move. Provider registration, or a one-line absence on the patient page.

Interactions and codes

Daily cost: a second screen outside Notya.

Now. No interaction pairs. No ICD-10-AM.

Wow move. A paid interaction source and an IHACPA licence, or those jobs stay outside the product.

Trust

Daily cost: a doctor will not bet a script on an unread tool.

Now. Ten Australia-only tools are on without a name beside them.

Wow move. A named GP and a named pharmacist on the unsigned list.

What to do next

Impress

A general-practice visit that records, drafts, and shows the NIP and the Poisons Standard without pretending to prescribe.

Velocity

The launch-boundary screen and the unsigned-tool list with two names. No new clinical engine.

Mandate

The lawyer packet, then My Health Record and eRx only if registration is actually open.

Already real, and worth keeping

  • The site answers: home, tools, medical, visit, today, and the patient page return 200. /au/doktor and /au/asistan return 404, which is correct. Those routes belong to Türkiye.
  • Australian role names, en-AU spelling, kilogram and centimetre, mmol/L, 12-hour time, and the state time zones are in the pack.
  • National Immunisation Program ticks, the notifiable-disease list, and the October 2026 Poisons Standard are on screen with attribution.
  • K10, adult BMI and waist, and an ECOG grade record are Australia’s own tools. Each licence notice was read. None has a clinician’s name.
  • Risky shared tools stay off: ESI, the BI-RADS report, and the KDIGO colour grids.
