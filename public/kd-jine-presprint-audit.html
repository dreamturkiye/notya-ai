Executive verdict

Obstetrics depth is already impressive for a Turkish EMR chapter (DÖB calendar, Anti-D, doğum spine, lohusa→bebek). Jinekoloji V1/V2 nails diagnosis frameworks (HPV tree, CYBH ön tanı, AUB/PALM-COEIN, KOK MEC). What will not make a jinekolog say “ulan bu başka” yet: treatment engines (STI regimens, menoraji ladder, EC), USG report+SUT speed, and live e-Doğum/e-rapor. Those three are the game-changers.
