Executive verdict

Notya is playing a different game than these three. Heidi, Lyrebird and Medow are ambient scribes that sit on top of Best Practice / Genie / Gentu / Cliniko and spit notes + letters back into that PMS. Notya is a full clinical practice application (patients, calendar, visit note with consent + approval, patient portal, invoices, clinic accounts, 44 specialty/profession roles, AU tools, named voice assistant). That is a real strategic advantage for greenfield and clinic-rebuild deals — and it is currently invisible in the market comparison frame doctors use when they shop “AI scribe”. Against Heidi/Lyrebird/Medow on their frame (scribe + letters + PMS sync), Notya today loses on every high-frequency buying criterion. Closing the six gaps below is how you pass them without abandoning the OS bet.

1. Who each product actually is

Heidi Health
Global scribe+
Ambient scribe + Evidence (cited clinical answers) + Heidi II agents/routines (morning prep, letters, tasks, forms). Free forever tier. Desktop app for telehealth audio. Clinician ~A$85/mo annual / A$120 monthly; Practice ~A$130 for team + full EHR. PMS sync (Cliniko/Nookal etc.) gated behind Practice. Huge brand; 175M+ interactions claimed.
Lyrebird Health
AU GP platform
Australian clinical AI: ambient consult + dictate → notes, referral/patient letters, WorkCover certificates, care plans & health assessments. Deep Best Practice integration (+ Cubiko, BetterConsult; Genie/Gentu also listed). Bp Free for eligible Bp users. Pro A$160/mo annual (A$240 monthly). Practice suites A$299–A$1,649 + GST with Document Sorter + real-time MBS prompts. Gold Coast HHS evaluation cited (100 clinicians, 7,499 consults).
Medow Health
Specialist suite
Specialist-clinic AI: dedicated model per specialty (50+), ambient scribe + Smart Dictation + Front Desk AI (phone → PMS booking). Bi-directional Genie (10-min), Gentu (minute), Elixir/Shexie/Meditech. GP/referral/patient letters + operation reports; anti-hallucination checks; ISO 27001. Quote-only pricing; 60-day money-back on first sub. Local CS configures templates before go-live.

Notya AU positioning (honest)

One product for the doctor’s day: record with consent → transcript → specialty-shaped draft → doctor approval; calendar; patient file; patient page (PIN); messages kept on-page; Medicare/PBS/GST checklists & link-outs; bring-your-own Stripe invoices; privacy request timer; multi-doctor clinic; live specialty-named assistant (voice). Self-serve 15-day trial, A$149/mo. Not a Best Practice plugin. Not a free forever scribe.

7. How to pass each competitor (playbooks)

Pass Heidi
  • Don’t fight Free forever on scribe-only — fight on OS completeness (portal + calendar + invoices).
  • Ship letters + annual price ladder so Clinician A$85 isn’t the only “serious” option.
  • Publish safety/approval story louder than Evidence theatre if you lack journal RAG yet.
  • Add telehealth capture so Desktop isn’t a hard block.
Pass Lyrebird
  • Priority #1 for GP market: Bp write-back (or lose every Bp clinic).
  • Letter + certificate pack; then MBS prompt assist.
  • Document Sorter is optional phase-2; letters+Bp close most deals.
  • Keep Notya OS as the “leave Bp eventually” upgrade path.
Pass Medow
  • Specialist letter pack (GP letter, referral, op report) with tone presets per specialty.
  • Genie/Gentu bi-directional for specialists who won’t migrate PMS.
  • Lean on Notya’s specialty role system + patient portal as the “suite” answer to Front Desk later.
  • White-glove onboarding for first 20 specialist clinics (match CS expectation).
