Executive verdict

Göz is a named specialty without a chapter. What exists is platform scaffolding: branş kaydı, TOD-first reference strings, a solid intake questionnaire, a SOAP prompt stub, reserved VA/GİB measurement type keys (not wired into any profile olcumler), dahiliye “yıllık göz dibi sevk”, and belge-ontology awareness (fundus/OCT/DR codes + goz router row with empty motors). There is no Göz hasta dosyası tab, no bilateral VA/GİB strip, no glokom/DR/katarakt/enjeksiyon engine, no OCT/fundus upload chips in imaging modalities, no prompts lock, no SGK GİL/anti-VEGF rapor, and no specialty Sağlığım module. A Turkish ophthalmologist on an 8-hour day gets the generic EMR spine — not ophthalmology. That is the honest pre-Claude baseline.

What exists vs what looks like product

Do not confuse platform stubs with a specialty

Belge ontology already knows FUN.DR0–4, glokom şüphesi, AMD, papilödem and routes “goz” to fundus/oct/dis_goz — but motors are empty and doctor imaging chips don’t list those modalities. VA/GİB keys exist in a TypeScript union for future chapters — they are not measurements a doctor can enter today. Inflating any of this to Strong would lie.
