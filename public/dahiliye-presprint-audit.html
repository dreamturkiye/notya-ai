Executive verdict

DAH-01 is a credible chronic-disease card skeleton: Uzlaşı 2025 HT classes/targets, TEMD DM cadence + annual tasks, lipid/tiroid mini, lab-grounded header chips, eGFR drug text warnings, red-flag gate, sevk with last panel. A Turkish internist will respect the safety posture (no fake SCORE2, no insulin titration, doctor locks). They will not say “ulan bu başka” yet. Versus KD/jine wow depth we are early: no SCORE2/KVR engine, no check-up package business ledger, no treatment dosing cards, no office GI/pulm/HF/CKD spines, no SGK rapor speed, no patient portal home-BP/glucose, no prompts/ persona lock. Closing that gap is the dahiliye wow sprint — not rewriting V1.

Three golden references (TR dahiliye desk)

Same trio locked in code as ref_code behind the clinician Kaynak toggle. Society guidelines hang off TEMD + Uzlaşı; MoH HYP is the public-sector twin.

1. TİHUD İç Hastalıkları (4. baskı)
Turkish specialty textbook. Defines the internist as chronic-disease manager + care coordinator — not hospitalist/ICU. Anchors check-up intervals, start-workups, sevk judgment.
2. Harrison İç Hastalıkları Prensipleri (TR)
Global gold standard used in TR teaching hospitals. Differential frameworks, red-flag thinking, comorbidity reasoning when TEMD is silent.
3. TEMD kılavuz ailesi + Uzlaşı 2025
TEMD DM 2026, Lipid 2021, Tiroid 2025, Obezite 2024, Osteo 2025 + Türk Hipertansiyon Uzlaşı Raporu 2025 (TİHUD+TEMD+TKD+TND+TAHUD+…). Office metabolic spine for private practice.

Deep dive — SCORE2 / lipid / check-up

Highest clinical + business gap cluster

HYP mandates SCORE2 for ≥40y (SCORE2-OP ≥70); TEMD lipid targets hang off risk category; private clinics sell check-up packages that SGK will not reimburse. V1 correctly refused a fake SCORE2 number — but without a reviewed formula + package ledger, dahiliye cannot match KD’s “thinks like my clinic” bar.
