Executive verdict

An Australian doctor opening any of the 44 offered roles does not get a specialty module at Türkiye pediatri depth. The Turkish chapter folders still sit in the monorepo, but AU builds only mount *.ulke.* routes — the pediatri engines (Neyzi, GİDR, M-CHAT, kohort UI) never appear on the AU visit. What AU does ship is real and correctly cautious: NIP antigen rows, AIR/PBS/Medicare/GST/Privacy ticks, Charlie/Clara specialty lines, Board-accurate role names, and several tools left off where licence or copyright blocks them. Ticks are notes; nothing is reported, claimed, or uploaded. Against the pediatri gold bar used for TR’s 40-branch parity work, every offered AU role is Thin. Closing this is a full-depth AU specialty closeout job (start with Paediatrics), not a rescore of the product-shell audit (mean 59 on 17 slices).
