Executive verdict

A beta Australian clinical dietitian can now do a real visit in Notya: write an ADIME-shaped note with PES, run MST / kJ·protein / IDDSI / labs / refeeding tools, see weight–waist–BMI over time, and leave a meal plan the patient can read (and diary into) on the portal. That clears the “actually useful for my daily work” bar. What still won’t make an APD say this is another product class: unsigned tools, 80-character meal lines, no food composition database, and a care-plan studio that is still just a review calendar.
