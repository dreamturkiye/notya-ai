Executive verdict

Göz is now a real chapter, not a baseline stub. On an 8-hour day the ophthalmologist opens the Göz tab and sees OD/OS VA with letter change, GİB against their own target, DR stage and the next injection in one strip. Entering both eyes takes one row, and last visit's values copy forward only after an explicit confirm. Glaucoma, DR (TEMD next to ICO, conflicts shown), anti-VEGF with the real SUT 4.2.33 gates, and the dahiliye göz dibi referral loop are working end to end in a smoke run. The patient's Sağlığım shows "Gözlerim", not pediatric growth curves. What still stops a "ulan bu başka" on imaging: Ayşe does not draft OCT/fundus reads yet and there is no side-by-side compare. On the SGK side, the GİL payment list and TOD guideline texts were not verifiable here, so those stay hekim-confirmed rather than embedded.
