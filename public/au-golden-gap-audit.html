Executive verdict

AU already looks like a real country product for roster, Medicare/PBS/MHR naming, references, shell chrome, and — uniquely — Clinical Dietitian tools/portal. It will not yet make a Turkish MD say the AU build matches their branş depth: one generic visit screen faces 321 TR specialty engines that exist in the repo but never mount in the AU build. The 2026-10-28 “44 Strong” rescore measured spine completeness (SOAP locks, danger flags, Triple Zero lines); this audit measures golden-standard clinical wow. Both can be true — do not conflate them.
