Executive verdict

A Turkish clinician opening Saç Ekimi, Estetik Cerrahi, Medikal Estetik, Klinik Dermatoloji, Longevity, Fizyoterapi, Klinik Psikoloji, Diyetisyen, Ergoterapi or Odyoloji gets one shared form shell, one local session ledger, and a reminder page in Sağlığım. Pediatri, on the same product, gets eight engines (growth LMS, vaccine plan, dose, development, well-child, cohort), eight test files, and six studios on the chart. The September 2026 hub that printed ~90–92 measured “a card and a date exist.” That number is not this bar, and it is not reused. The ten branches are not empty: each has a real deterministic function, a gated portal route, a cohort list, and a KVKK checklist. They are not at the gold reference, and none is ahead of it. Medikal estetik leads only because the vascular flag is a real safety function. Ergoterapi and fizyoterapi sit at the bottom because the instrument is a checkbox or a required diagnosis string plus a date. Raising them means their own instruments, still gated, and still not a copy of TUS dermatoloji, FTR, psikiyatri, KBB or plastik Yaram.

What a clinic day still costs

The form does not know the patient every visit
The hasta page passes patientId. KlinikBolumHome drops it. Greft, soğuma, ICF and PTA are retyped every time. Pediatri studios read the child on the chart.
The session ledger is the browser record
Seans rows and “iki nüsha rıza” live in local storage. A second device, a colleague, or a cleared browser loses the consent flag. Gold chapters write the visit.
Sağlığım shows a fixed rhythm portal
All ten pages are gated and patient-safe. None of them prints that patient’s dates. The parent-facing wow on pediatri is the child’s own plan; here the subtitle is the whole module.
112 is a sentence safety
Medikal estetik can flag vision loss or livedo. The psychologist crisis path returns the 112 text. Nothing sticks an acil band on the chart the way the derm or pediatri chapters do.
