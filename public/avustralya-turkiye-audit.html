Executive verdict

An Australian doctor can sign in, keep a calendar, open a patient, record a visit, and approve a note. That shell is real and it is in English. It is not the Turkey product with the labels translated. The 30 specialty chapters and the 10 Özel Branşlar that make Turkey the reference sit in the Australia repository and do not open from /visit. The assistant is off. Medicare is a free-text label, not an Individual Healthcare Identifier. PBS and Medicare are checklists, not claims. Nothing in this pack has been read by an Australian clinician or lawyer. That is why the mean stays at 48.

How this was scored

Turkey is 100 on each row as the reference that exists, including slices that are Turkey-only by law. Australia is scored on the country-adapted equivalent a doctor can actually open, not on files that the country build never mounts. Strong ≥85, Partial 45–84, Thin 15–44, Missing <15. Medula, e-Nabız, e-Reçete, SGK, MBYS, NVI, e-Fatura and KVKK wording are exempt: they must not be translated. Their absence is not a deduction. The missing Australian equivalent is.

Roles are not a translation of the Turkish 40

The landing says “40 specialties”. The pack’s own role list is a different forty. It adds cardio-thoracic as its own line, vascular surgery, radiation oncology, geriatric medicine, immunology and allergy, haematology, pain medicine, podiatry and speech pathology. It does not have a clinic-dermatology role. Hair transplantation, cosmetic surgery, non-surgical cosmetic procedures, longevity, physiotherapy, psychology, dietetics, occupational therapy and audiology are names on that list. They are not the Turkey studios.
