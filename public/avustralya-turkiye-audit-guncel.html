What moved

Immunisation 50 → 70
Antigen names were already on the age rows. Doctors now tick the report particulars section 10 of the rules prescribes: who was vaccinated, the day, the brand, dose number and batch, and the time and form of the report. The upload conditions now follow the compilation in force on 1 July 2026. Sections 23 and 24 of the My Health Records Rules 2026 name the record kind, and the case where fees are not more than 6 months overdue. A tick is a note. Nothing is uploaded. A photo is still not read.
